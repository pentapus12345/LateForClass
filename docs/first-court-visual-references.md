# First Court — visual target reference sheet

**Purpose:** A compact, visual art-direction brief for the existing seven-room First Court. The user wants the atmosphere of richly lived-in Oxford collegiate interiors: old timber, portraits, textured plaster, restrained rugs, school paraphernalia, books and welcoming pockets of warm light. This is the visual **target**, not a list of assets already available or an instruction to copy any photograph exactly.

## Reference A — panelled academic hall and portrait gallery

**[Eleanor Lodge, Lady Margaret Hall — 5th Studio (photographs)](https://www.5thstudio.co.uk/projects/eleanor-lodge-lady-margaret-hall/)**

Look for: wood-panelled lower walls and gallery, framed portraits placed with intention, warm timber against lighter surfaces, structural beams meeting columns cleanly. **Apply to:** grand reception/gallery in a restrained form, with *one* characteristic portrait and appropriately scaled panelling. This is **not** a demand to add long dining tables to reception.

**[New College, Oxford — history and hall photography](https://www.new.ox.ac.uk/new-college-history)**

Look for: centuries of institutional portraits, dark wood surfaces and an inhabited historic setting. **Apply to:** selected memorial/portrait walls and the woodwork language.

## Reference B — scholarly reading room and books

**[Duke Humfrey's Library, Bodleian Libraries — Wikimedia photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Duke_Humfrey%27s_Library_Interior_1,_Bodleian_Library,_Oxford,_UK_-_Diliff.jpg)**

Look for: dark wood bookcases, layered academic interiors, distinctive ceilings, naturally lit window end. **Apply to:** Room 15 Reading Room and the quieter side of Scholars' Passage, scaled way down from a major library to one modest room.

## Reference C — first-person school prototype already established

**[Historic visual slice — this repository](historic-slice.md)**

Look for: **limewashed plaster over oak wainscoting, worn timber boards, an existing portrait, college noticeboard, stone stair and lanterns**. This is the most practical style reference because the project already includes some of the required systems and assets. **Apply to:** Scholars' Passage first; adapt it to actual passage geometry rather than copying a corridor footprint.

**[Historic-school reference and material kit — this repository](historic-school-reference.md)** · **[Catalog and kit documentation](historic-asset-kit.md)**

Look for: approved masonry, cloister, window and room proportions, source/attribution notes and the runtime model catalog. Do not introduce a different style that conflicts with these references.

## Reference D — cloister, stone and courtyard

**[New College cloister, original Wikimedia reference](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:New_College_cloisters.jpg)** · **[New College quadrangle](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:New_College_quad.jpg)**

Look for: real support at arches, stone course size, rhythm, convincing recesses, stone around open court, modest exterior context. **Apply to:** West Cloister, exposed vault-end repairs and courtyard finish. Keep this area more austere than the timber-lined interior passage.

## Reference E — rugs and room warmth

**Direction:** Aged, muted patterned textiles (burgundy/russet/dull blue/cream; no bright geometric carpet) in enclosed passages or furnished rooms. Use small, low-cost carpet/runner geometry or approved textures with safe separation above the floor; avoid overlapping coplanar faces and unnecessary shadows. The rug should be plausible in the quiet academic interior, **not** the exposed cloister. This is a textual art-direction reference; **no specific photographed rug or licensed texture is bundled**.

## What to copy — and what not to copy

| Copy the *character* | Do not copy |
|---|---|
| Aged, varied timber and stone | Large dining-hall furniture in small classrooms |
| Portraits as institutional history | An identical painting on every wall |
| Warm, human-scale wall treatments under grand vaults | Fully wood-panelled exterior cloister |
| Soft runners and rugs in sheltered interiors | Coplanar overlays that shimmer or block walking |
| Books, practical furniture, signs, lamps, paperwork | Clutter obstructing doors or route decisions |
| Distinctive historic building parts with plausible joints | Floating arch segments or abruptly open vaults |

**Attribution and redistribution:** The linked third-party photographs are **links only**; no external photographs are included in this downloadable package. Follow original authors' usage terms before making derivative assets from a photograph. For actual game textures/models, continue the project's existing source and license record procedures.
